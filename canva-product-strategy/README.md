# Canva Product Strategy Analysis

## Overview

An independent product strategy analysis of Canva, created as a
product management case study for educational and portfolio purposes.

## Objective

The objective of this analysis is to understand Canva's current
competitive position, identify potential strategic gaps, and propose
product opportunities that could strengthen its long-term growth.

## Areas Explored

- Market and competitive landscape
- Canva's current product strategy
- AI and Generative AI positioning
- Competitive analysis
- Strategic product gaps
- Growth opportunities
- Product recommendations
- Success metrics

## Key Strategic Insight

One potential strategic gap identified is AI-generated output quality.

As AI becomes increasingly important to design workflows, users may
begin comparing platforms not only on ease of use and templates, but
also on the quality, consistency and controllability of AI-generated
outputs.

This creates an opportunity for Canva to leverage Leonardo.Ai as a
deeper strategic capability and potential competitive advantage.
